# Remote Work Policy

Effective date: 2024-03-01
Owner: People Operations

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how employees of Northwind Analytics may work remotely while maintaining collaboration, data security, and customer service. It applies to all employees, except those in roles designated On-Site in the job description.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Tenure Requirement

Employees become eligible for remote or hybrid work after completing 90 calendar days of employment. Before that, employees work on-site unless their manager and People Operations agree otherwise in writing.

### 2.2 Role Classification

Each role is classified as Remote, Hybrid, or On-Site by the hiring department and People Operations. The classification appears in the job description and is reviewed every January.

## 3. Work Schedule

### 3.1 Core Hours

All remote and hybrid employees must be available from 10:00 to 15:00 local time, Monday through Friday, for meetings and colleague requests. Outside core hours, employees may keep a flexible schedule agreed with their manager.

### 3.2 Hybrid On-Site Requirement

Employees in hybrid roles must be on-site at least 2 days per week. Each team selects its on-site days, and managers publish them by the last business day of the preceding month. Exceptions require approval under Section 6.2.

## 4. Equipment and Reimbursements

### 4.1 General Rules

Equipment bought under this section remains company property. Employees must return it within 14 days of separation if requested. Replacement of failed company-issued laptops is handled by IT and does not count against any stipend.

### 4.2 Equipment Stipend

Eligible employees receive a one-time home office equipment stipend of $750 toward items such as a desk, chair, monitor, or keyboard. Receipts must be submitted through the expense system within 60 days of purchase. The stipend is not renewed if an employee changes roles or switches between remote and hybrid status.

### 4.3 Internet Reimbursement

Remote and hybrid employees may claim $50 per month toward home internet service using Form RW-7. Claims must be submitted by the fifth business day of the following month. Claims filed more than 60 days after the service month are not paid. Approved amounts are included in the next payroll cycle.

## 5. Security

### 5.1 VPN Use

Employees must connect to the company VPN whenever they access internal systems, customer data, or source code repositories from outside a Northwind Analytics office. Use of public Wi-Fi without the VPN is prohibited.

### 5.2 Devices and Data

Work must be performed on company-managed laptops with screen lock set to 5 minutes or less. Peripherals purchased under Section 4.2 must not be used to store customer data. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 2 hours of discovery.

## 6. Approval Process

### 6.1 Submitting a Request

After meeting the requirement in Section 2.1, the employee submits Form RW-3 to their manager. The manager has 5 business days to review and forward it to People Operations. People Operations issues a final decision within 5 further business days.

### 6.2 Exceptions and Changes

A new Form RW-3 is required to change a work arrangement, to request an exception to Section 3.2, or to work from a different state. Working from outside the country for more than 10 days in a calendar year requires written approval from both People Operations and Legal.

## 7. Compliance

### 7.1 Review and Revocation

Managers review each arrangement every 6 months. People Operations may withdraw approval with 30 days' written notice if an employee does not meet the core hours in Section 3.1 or the on-site requirement in Section 3.2. Approval may be withdrawn immediately for a violation of Section 5.

### 7.2 Questions

Direct questions about this policy to People Operations through the HR Help Desk. Employees should not assume an exception has been granted until they receive written confirmation.
