# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Effective date: 2022-01-01
Owner: People Operations

## 1. Purpose

Northwind Analytics provides paid time off so that employees can rest, recharge, and attend to personal matters. This policy explains how PTO is earned, carried over, requested, and used, and how it relates to company holidays and sick leave.

## 2. Scope and Eligibility

### 2.1 Covered Employees

This policy applies to all regular full-time employees of Northwind Analytics. Contractors, interns, and temporary workers are not eligible for PTO.

### 2.2 Probation Period

New hires become eligible to use PTO only after completing a 90-day probation period. Requests for dates that fall within the probation period will not be approved. Allowance for the first year is calculated under Section 3.2.

## 3. Annual Allowance

### 3.1 Full-Time Allowance

Full-time employees receive 15 days of PTO per year, credited on 1 January.

### 3.2 Partial Year

Employees who join during the year receive a prorated allowance based on the whole months remaining in the calendar year. For example, an employee who starts on 1 July receives 7.5 days for that year.

### 3.3 Tracking

PTO is recorded in the HR system in increments of half a day. Employees can view their balance at any time in the HR portal.

## 4. Carryover

### 4.1 Maximum Carryover

Unused PTO may be carried over up to a maximum of 10 days into the next year. Carried-over days are added to the new year's allowance on 1 January.

### 4.2 Forfeiture

Any unused PTO above the 10-day limit is forfeited at the close of business on 31 December. Forfeited days are not paid out and cannot be reinstated.

### 4.3 Example

An employee with 13 unused days on 31 December carries over 10 days and forfeits 3 days. The employee starts the new year with 25 days (10 carried over plus 15 newly credited).

## 5. Requesting PTO

### 5.1 Form PTO-1

PTO requests are made with Form PTO-1, submitted to the employee's manager at least 5 business days in advance of the first requested day.

### 5.2 Approval

Managers will approve or decline a request within 3 business days of receiving Form PTO-1, based on team coverage and project deadlines. Requests longer than 10 consecutive days also require approval from the department head.

### 5.3 Short-Notice Requests

When an unforeseen personal matter prevents 5 business days of notice, the employee must contact their manager as soon as possible. Approval is at the manager's discretion, and Form PTO-1 must be submitted within 2 business days of the employee's return.

### 5.4 Cancellations

Employees who cancel approved PTO must notify their manager in writing. Cancelled days are returned to the employee's balance.

## 6. Company Holidays

### 6.1 Holiday Schedule

Northwind Analytics observes 9 paid company holidays per year. People Operations publishes the schedule by 1 December for the following year.

### 6.2 Relationship to PTO

Company holidays are not deducted from the PTO balance. If a holiday falls within an approved PTO period, only the non-holiday workdays are charged under Section 3.3.

## 7. Sick Leave

### 7.1 Allowance

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Each full-time employee receives 5 days of sick leave per year, and sick days are never deducted from the PTO balance described in Section 3.

### 7.2 Reporting an Absence

Employees must notify their manager before the start of their workday when taking sick leave. For absences of more than 3 consecutive days, employees must submit Form SL-2 with supporting documentation to People Operations.

### 7.3 Extended Illness

If sick leave is exhausted, an employee may request to use accrued PTO with manager approval, subject to the eligibility rules in Section 2.2.

## 8. Separation from the Company

### 8.1 Payout of Unused PTO

Employees who have completed the probation period and leave the company are paid for unused PTO at their final base daily rate in the final paycheck. Unused sick leave is not paid out.

## 9. Administration

### 9.1 Questions

Questions about this policy should be directed to People Operations through the HR portal. People Operations responds within 2 business days.

### 9.2 Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Chief People Officer and will be recorded in the employee's file.
