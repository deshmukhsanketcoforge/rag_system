# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Effective date: 2024-01-15
Owner: Finance

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Northwind Analytics reimburses employees for reasonable business expenses paid out of pocket. It ensures that expenses are documented, approved, and repaid consistently and promptly.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time, part-time, and fixed-term employees. It applies to contractors only when their agreement expressly allows reimbursement. All expenses must be incurred for Northwind Analytics business and must not fall under Section 7.

## 3. General Requirements

### 3.1 Business Purpose

Every expense must have a clear business purpose, which the employee states on the claim. Employees are expected to choose cost-effective options and to spend company funds as carefully as their own.

### 3.2 Receipts

An itemized receipt is required for any expense over $25. For expenses of $25 or less, no receipt is needed, but the claim must list the date, vendor, and amount. If a required receipt is lost, the employee must complete Form EX-15 (Missing Receipt Declaration), and the approver named in Section 5.3 must sign it.

## 4. Travel Expenses

### 4.1 Transportation

Employees should book economy airfare and standard-class rail at least 14 days before departure whenever possible. Use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile. Taxis and rideshares are reimbursable when public transport is impractical.

### 4.2 Lodging

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $220 per night. In New York, San Francisco, and London the limit is $300 per night. Employees must book rooms through the corporate travel portal where available.

### 4.3 Meal Per-Diem

When travelling overnight on company business, employees receive a meal per-diem of $60 per day. The per-diem covers breakfast, lunch, dinner, and tips. Receipts are not required for per-diem amounts, but alcohol is never covered (see Section 7.1).

## 5. Submission and Approval

### 5.1 Submitting Form EX-14

All expenses must be submitted on Form EX-14 through the Finance portal. Receipts required under Section 3.2 must be attached to the form as legible scans or photographs.

### 5.2 Submission Deadline

Form EX-14 must be submitted within 30 days after the date of the expense. For travel, the 30 days run from the date each expense was incurred, not from the date of return. Claims submitted after the deadline are not reimbursed unless the Finance Director grants a written exception.

### 5.3 Approval Limits

Claims totalling up to $1,000 require approval from the employee's manager. Claims above $1,000 require approval from the employee's department director. Employees may not approve their own claims, and claims must not be split into smaller amounts to avoid a higher approval level.

## 6. Reimbursement Timeline

Finance reviews each approved Form EX-14 within 3 business days of receipt. Approved claims are paid by direct deposit within 10 business days of Finance receiving a complete, approved form. Incomplete claims are returned to the employee with an explanation, and the 10-business-day period restarts when the corrected form is resubmitted. Claims rejected under Section 5.2 are not paid.

## 7. Non-Reimbursable Items

### 7.1 Personal and Discretionary Expenses

The following are not reimbursable under any circumstances:

- Alcohol, including alcohol bought with meals
- Personal entertainment, such as movies, hotel pay-per-view, and sporting events
- Traffic fines, parking violations, and vehicle repair costs
- Personal items, including clothing, toiletries, and grooming services
- Costs added by personal travel extensions or travel companions
- Gym memberships and childcare

### 7.2 Unsupported Claims

Expenses over $25 without a receipt or a signed Form EX-15, expenses without the required approval under Section 5.3, and expenses submitted after the deadline in Section 5.2 are not reimbursable.

## 8. Violations and Questions

Submitting false or inflated claims is a serious violation of company policy. It may result in repayment of the amounts involved and disciplinary action, up to and including termination. Questions about this policy should be sent to the Finance team at expenses@northwindanalytics.com.
