# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Effective date: 2024-01-01
Owner: People Operations

## 1. Purpose

Northwind Analytics encourages employees to rest and recharge. This policy explains how paid time off is earned, carried over, requested, and approved for all employees of the company.

## 2. Scope and Definitions

### 2.1 Who Is Covered

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Northwind Analytics. Contractors and temporary agency staff are not eligible for PTO.

### 2.2 Definitions

- **PTO day:** one scheduled working day of paid absence for vacation, personal matters, or other time away that is not sick leave.
- **Business day:** Monday through Friday, excluding the company holidays described in Section 6.1.
- **Leave year:** January 1 through December 31.

## 3. Annual Allowance

### 3.1 Full-Time Employees

Full-time employees receive 20 days of PTO per leave year. PTO is credited to the employee's balance in the HR system and is visible in the employee portal at any time.

### 3.2 New Hire Accrual

New hires accrue PTO monthly starting from their first day of employment. Accrual is calculated at one-twelfth of the annual allowance per month, so a new hire's first-year balance depends on their start date. Accrued days may be used as they are earned, subject to manager approval under Section 5.

## 4. Carryover

### 4.1 Carryover Limit

Unused PTO may be carried over into the next leave year up to a maximum of 5 days. Any balance above 5 days on December 31 is forfeited.

### 4.2 Use of Carried-Over Days

Carried-over days are added to the new year's balance on January 1. Employees are encouraged to schedule them early in the year. Carryover does not change the annual allowance described in Section 3.1.

## 5. Requesting PTO

### 5.1 Short Absences

For absences of fewer than 3 days, employees should request approval from their manager through the HR system. Managers should respond within 3 business days.

### 5.2 Absences of 3 Days or More

For absences of 3 days or more, employees must submit Form PTO-1 at least 10 business days in advance. Requests submitted with less notice may be declined unless the circumstances are exceptional and the manager and People Operations both approve them under Section 8.2.

### 5.3 Approval and Coverage

Managers review each request against team workload and coverage. A request is not approved until the employee receives written confirmation, which may be an email or a status update in the HR system. Employees should not book non-refundable travel before receiving confirmation.

## 6. Company Holidays and Sick Leave

### 6.1 Company Holidays

Northwind Analytics observes 10 paid company holidays per year. The holiday calendar is published by People Operations each December for the following year. Company holidays do not reduce an employee's PTO balance.

### 6.2 Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 8 days of sick leave per year, and sick days are never deducted from the PTO balance. Sick leave does not require Form PTO-1. Employees should notify their manager as early as possible on the first day of absence.

## 7. Separation from the Company

### 7.1 Final Balance

When employment ends, People Operations calculates the employee's final PTO balance as of the last working day.

### 7.2 Negative Balances

If an employee has used more PTO than they have accrued, People Operations will review the difference with the employee. Any deduction from final pay will be made only where permitted by law.

### 7.3 Payout

Accrued but unused PTO is paid out at separation where required by applicable law. Sick leave and company holidays are not paid out.

## 8. Questions and Exceptions

### 8.1 Contact

Questions about this policy should be sent to People Operations at peopleops@northwindanalytics.example. Employees may also review their balance in the employee portal.

### 8.2 Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy, including late Form PTO-1 submissions and carryover above the limit in Section 4.1, require written approval from the Director of People Operations. Approved exceptions are documented in the employee's file and do not set a precedent for future requests.
