# Information Security Policy
Effective date: 2024-02-01
Owner: Security Team

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets the minimum security requirements that protect Northwind Analytics systems, customer data, and employee information. It supports our contractual commitments to customers and our obligations under applicable data protection law.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and interns at Northwind Analytics, and to any device or account used to access company systems. Managers are responsible for ensuring their teams follow it.

## 3. Account Security
### 3.1 Passwords
Passwords for company accounts must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords must not be reused across systems or shared with anyone, including colleagues and IT staff. Use the company-approved password manager to generate and store them. Any password suspected to be compromised must be changed within 24 hours.

### 3.2 Multi-Factor Authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required on all company accounts, including email, source code repositories, cloud consoles, and HR systems. Authenticator apps and hardware keys are preferred. SMS codes are permitted only when no other method is available. A lost or stolen MFA device must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours.

## 4. Device Security
### 4.1 Laptop Encryption
Full-disk encryption is mandatory on all company laptops and must be enabled before a laptop is issued. IT verifies encryption at setup and audits all devices quarterly. Employees must not disable encryption, and must configure screens to lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.

### 4.2 Personal and Mobile Devices
Personal phones may access company email only if they are enrolled in mobile device management, protected by a PIN of at least six digits or biometrics, and running a supported operating system version. Personal laptops must not store Confidential or Restricted data as defined in Section 5.1.

## 5. Data Classification and Handling
### 5.1 Classifications
Company information is assigned one of four classifications:

- **Public:** approved for external release, such as published marketing material.
- **Internal:** general business information not intended for outsiders, such as organization charts.
- **Confidential:** customer data, source code, unreleased financial results, and employee records.
- **Restricted:** authentication secrets, encryption keys, and regulated data such as payment card or health information.

Unlabeled information is treated as Internal until the data owner classifies it.

### 5.2 Handling Requirements
Confidential data must be stored only in company-approved systems and encrypted in transit and at rest. Restricted data may be accessed only by named individuals approved by the Security Team and must never be sent by email or chat. Neither Confidential nor Restricted data may be copied to personal cloud storage or removable media. Customer data must be deleted within 90 days of contract termination unless the law requires longer retention.

## 6. Incident Response
### 6.1 Incident Reporting
Anyone who suspects a security incident must report it within 4 hours of discovery. Examples include a lost device, a clicked phishing link, unauthorized access, or accidental data exposure. Submit Form SEC-2 through the Security Portal. For urgent events, also call the Security Team hotline at extension 5500. Do not attempt to investigate on your own, and do not delete logs, messages, or other evidence.

### 6.2 Investigation and Notification
The Security Team acknowledges each report within 1 hour during business hours and assigns a severity rating. If customer data is affected, the Legal team notifies the affected customers within 72 hours of confirming the incident. Employees who report in good faith will not be penalized, even if the report proves to be a false alarm.

## 7. Training and Compliance
### 7.1 Annual Security Training
All personnel must complete security awareness training within 30 days of their start date and once every 12 months afterward. The Security Team tracks completion and runs phishing simulations each quarter. Accounts of personnel more than 14 days overdue on training may be suspended until it is completed.

### 7.2 Exceptions and Enforcement
Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Security Team on Form SEC-5 and expire after 90 days unless renewed. Violations, including failure to report an incident as required by Section 6.1, may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment. Questions about this policy should be directed to the Security Team.
